## English Long Profile

Hitori Jigyo Lab is a Japanese-language media site operated by TATSU. The audience is Japanese solo business owners, freelancers, side-business builders, creators, and small local operators who want to build a small, practical business with AI and SaaS tools.

The site focuses on hands-on testing and decision support rather than generic rankings. I review AI tools, overseas SaaS products, no-code and website builders, ecommerce platforms, newsletter tools, accounting tools, and business workflows. For overseas products, I pay special attention to the points Japanese users care about before subscribing: Japanese-language usability, pricing converted to yen, free plan limits, payment methods, cancellation flow, support, and whether ChatGPT or Codex can replace part of the workflow.

Content formats include practical reviews, comparison tables, setup notes, experiment logs, diagnosis pages, and honest recommendations based on real testing. The editorial goal is to help Japanese solo operators choose tools carefully and avoid unnecessary subscriptions.
