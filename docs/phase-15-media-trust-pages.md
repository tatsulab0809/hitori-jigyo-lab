# Phase 15: Media trust pages

## Purpose

Strengthen Hitori Jigyo Lab as a reviewable affiliate media property before additional overseas affiliate applications.

## Changes

- Added a visible operations/proof block to the home page.
- Added reviewer-oriented links from the home page to the media overview, review policy, and affiliate disclosure.
- Expanded `/for-partners/` with a media snapshot, positioning, prohibited promotion methods, reader journey, and partnership fit.
- Expanded `/review-policy/` with hands-on/research/open-item separation, ad-link separation, and update policy.

## Reasoning

The site already has many articles and comparison pages. The next approval bottleneck is not just volume, but whether a partner manager can quickly understand the site, its audience, its promotional methods, and its editorial standards.
