# ひとり事業ラボ 画像方針

## Core Direction

Use a simple, slightly awkward, friendly 4-koma manga style.

The site should not use sleek SaaS stock images, glossy modern lifestyle photos, or overly polished anime art as the primary visual identity.

Preferred impression:

> 昭和生まれの事業主が、AIや海外SaaSにちょっと戸惑いながら向き合う実験メモ漫画。

## Visual Rules

- Use thick black outlines, flat colors, and simple expressions.
- A little clumsy and charming is better than stylish and perfect.
- Characters may vary from image to image; the face does not need to be identical every time.
- Keep the tone warm, practical, and slightly humorous.
- Use modest real-life scenes: desk, laptop, notebook, coffee, sticky notes, invoices, tool cards, simple app screens.
- Color base: navy, muted gold, warm off-white, pale blue, soft green, small red accents.
- Do not add readable text inside images unless absolutely necessary.
- Do not use brand logos or product UI that looks like a real service screen.
- Avoid hard-selling affiliate visuals.
- Avoid abstract corporate gradients, luxury workspace photos, glossy SaaS dashboards, and generic business stock photos.
- Avoid overly beautiful anime characters; TATSU should feel approachable rather than heroic.

## Layout Rules

- Article eyecatches should usually be landscape, around 1200 x 700.
- A 4-panel comic layout is preferred for important hub pages and experiment logs.
- Single-scene comic images are acceptable for simple articles.
- Keep the subject readable at thumbnail size.
- Do not reuse the exact same composition repeatedly.

## Existing Asset Direction

- Existing SVG images that already feel simple and comic-like can remain temporarily.
- Polished PNG images should be replaced gradually with the new 4-koma manga style.
- Top-page cards and future overseas SaaS content should use the new style first.
