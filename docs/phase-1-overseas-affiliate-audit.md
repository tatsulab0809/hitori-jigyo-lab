# Phase 1: Overseas Affiliate Direction Audit

Date: 2026-08-26

## Current Decision

Use `hitori-jigyo-lab.com` as the main affiliate business hub.

Do not restart from zero. The existing site already has the right trust base:

- A real operator voice: TATSU
- A clear experiment-log concept
- A public domain
- Astro implementation
- Legal and trust pages
- Early articles with real process notes

The next business direction should be:

> Overseas AI/SaaS tools tested from the viewpoint of a Japanese solo business owner.

This keeps the original "ひとり事業ラボ" concept, but shifts the revenue engine from general domestic affiliate content to higher-value overseas SaaS and tool referrals.

## Editorial Rules For Overseas Affiliate Content

Every overseas SaaS article must answer:

1. What problem does this solve for a solo operator?
2. Did TATSU actually try it, register it, or inspect the official information?
3. Can it be used comfortably from Japan?
4. What does it cost in approximate yen?
5. Is there a free plan or trial?
6. How hard is cancellation?
7. Can ChatGPT/Codex replace it?
8. Who should not use it?
9. What is TATSU's honest current judgment?

Do not publish generic "Top 10" articles unless they include original testing, screenshots, decision notes, or a useful interactive tool.
