## Rule For Future Articles

Every new overseas SaaS review should use:

1. A manga-style eyecatch.
2. Real screenshots only when they clarify registration, pricing, cancellation, or setup.
3. Simple diagrams or tables for comparisons.

Screenshots should be practical evidence, not decorative visuals.
