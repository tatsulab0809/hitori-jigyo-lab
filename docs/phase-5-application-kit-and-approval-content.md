## Why This Matters

Affiliate networks and SaaS programs often review:

- who owns the site,
- what audience the site serves,
- how links will be promoted,
- whether there is real content,
- whether disclosure and compliance pages exist,
- whether the site looks like a coupon/spam property.

Hitori Jigyo Lab should present itself as a Japanese practical lab for solo operators, not as a generic AI-generated affiliate catalog.
