# ひとり事業ラボ Site Structure

## Purpose

This file defines the minimum site structure needed before affiliate service provider registration.

## Minimum Public Site

The first public version should include:

- Top page
- Article list
- Article detail pages
- Category pages
- About page
- Operator information page
- Privacy policy
- Disclaimer
- Affiliate disclosure
- Contact page

## URL Structure

### Main Pages

- `/`
- `/articles/`
- `/categories/`
- `/about/`
- `/operator/`
- `/privacy-policy/`
- `/disclaimer/`
- `/affiliate-disclosure/`
- `/contact/`

### Category Pages

- `/categories/ai-tools/`
- `/categories/experiment-log/`
- `/categories/solo-business-tools/`
- `/categories/roadmap/`
- `/categories/workspace/`

### Article Pages

Use:

- `/articles/{slug}/`

Initial examples:

- `/articles/about-hitori-jigyo-lab/`
- `/articles/monthly-50000-roadmap/`
- `/articles/build-affiliate-site-with-codex/`
- `/articles/chatgpt-plus-for-side-business/`
- `/articles/ai-tools-for-side-business/`

## Top Page Sections

1. Hero
2. Latest experiment logs
3. Monthly 50,000 yen roadmap
4. Tool comparison categories
5. First-time reader guide
6. Operator profile

## Article Template

Each article should include:

- Title
- Description
- Published date
- Updated date
- Category
- Affiliate disclosure when needed
- Summary
- Main content
- TATSU note
- Related articles
- CTA

## Experiment Log Template

Experiment articles should include:

- What was tested
- Why it was tested
- Tools used
- Starting assumptions
- Actual process
- Cost
- Time spent
- Result
- What worked
- What did not work
- What to try next
- TATSU's honest note

## Comparison Article Template

Comparison articles should include:

- Who the comparison is for
- Quick recommendation
- Comparison table
- Main differences
- Pros and cons
- Pricing note
- Recommended choice by user type
- TATSU's current choice
- Official links or affiliate links
- Update date and checking note

## Legal And Trust Pages

### About

Explain the purpose of the site and what readers can expect.

### Operator

Use the semi-anonymous TATSU profile.

### Privacy Policy

Needed for Analytics, contact forms, affiliate links, and ad-related services.

### Disclaimer

Clarify that results are not guaranteed and that tool information may change.

### Affiliate Disclosure

Clearly state that the site may include affiliate links.

### Contact

Initially, a simple email address or form placeholder is enough.

## Design Direction

Recommended direction:

- Practical, calm, and readable
- More like a workbench than a flashy marketing site
- Slight lab feeling, but not too playful
- Clear navigation
- Strong article readability
- Useful comparison tables

Avoid:

- Overly AI-looking neon visuals
- Generic corporate SaaS style
- Hard-selling affiliate design
- Too much decorative styling
